Although we have survived Trump’s frontal assault on the US Post Office, it’s still in a very fragile state. It remains the only federal agency required to keep it’s entire retirement budget readily available in liquid form on reserve.

As Richard John has argued….

Many have recognized the Post Office’s role in the formation of our democracy - the special flat rates for political pamphlets and newspapers enabled their distribution.

My idea for saving the post office is relatively simple, and merely extends a feature we have become quite familiar with in the world of digital delivery - I call it “Deliver Upon”, although “Scheduled Delivery” is a bit more catchy.
