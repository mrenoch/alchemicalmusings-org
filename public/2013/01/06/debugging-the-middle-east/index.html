Debugging the Middle East

January 6, 2013 · Uncategorized
http://alchemicalmusings.org/2009/01/19/the-tweets-of-war/
I’ve been thinking about the impact of open source technologies on insurgency tactics, probably triggered by the war in the middle east.  In particular, I am wondering about the impact if tools such as 3D printing (http://bits.blogs.nytimes.com/2012/10/07/with-a-3-d-printer-building-a-gun-at-home/), open source drones, open source rocket/missile guidance systems (http://www.altusmetrum.org/), and arduinos (http://www.arduino.cc/). The arduinos in particular seem innocuous enough, but I think they can probably be used to build vicious IEDs – ones that turn IED manufacture from a hardware problem into a software one, and spawn a new generation of bomb-kiddies. The designs will be uploaded to pirate bay, and IED evolution will occur at the speed of the internet. Think of the capabilities. Modular, library-driven sensing - that means, face recognition, humidity/light detection, precise scheduling, networking, and even randomness. Truly, the Joker’s detonators.
