![apple tree](/images/2006/12/apple_tree_1.jpg)In keeping with the Alchemist's recent "[free](http://alchemicalmusings.org/2006/11/16/free-energy/)" disambuguation theme, here is my latest installment on the OLPC project:

**[Free Laptops:](http://pocketknowledge.tc.columbia.edu/home.php/viewfile/download/23438)**
**[Creating, Producing and Sharing a Revolution](http://pocketknowledge.tc.columbia.edu/home.php/viewfile/download/23438)**

In this essay/story I leave wise 'ol [Plato](http://alchemicalmusings.org/2006/11/03/plato-and-the-laptop/) behind, and tried for a straight up, journalistic take on the project. Except there is no such thing as objectivity in journalism, so in this piece is explicitly infused with subjectivity and ideology. [Conversations](http://blog.ianbicking.org/nonlinear-learning-nonlinear-internet.html) with Ian Bicking helped convince me that believing in this project is a ultimately a matter of faith, in which case our optimism or cynicism go a long way towards shaping reality. And our perceptions are often shaped by media, so lets start advocating for this project instead of kicking it in the shins.This is one reason I am starting to think that [olpcnews](http://www.olpcnews.com/prototypes/olpc/low_cost_computing.html) should seriously ease up on the project, stop taking cheap swipes and jibes, and start offering more constructive criticism, or even better, apply for some grants so they can fix the project as they see fit.
Happy Holidays!
