![Socrates](/images/2006/11/250px-Socrates_Louvre.jpg "Socrates")Well, midterms have come and gone, and somehow I managed to complete my two papers on time, somewhere between San Francisco and [PloneCon](http://plone.org/events/conferences/seattle-2006/agenda) in Seattle.
In my class on the Social Impact of Mass Media I was really impressed with Peter's [Speaking into the Air](http://www.press.uchicago.edu/cgi-bin/hfs.cgi/00/13802.ctl), and wanted to revisit the Phaedrus. While reading it I was making connections to [read-only/read-write culture](http://wikimania2006.wikimedia.org/wiki/Archives#Lawrence_Lessig_-_The_Ethics_of_the_Free_Culture_Movement), and wanted to explore that connection to Plato's analysis of writing. Also, his conversation has everything in the world to do with my thinking on the effects of Technology on Epistomology itself, and Memory [in particular](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/draft/jonah/threatnyouth/html/threatnyouth_permanentrecord.html).
Still, when I sat down to write the paper, I kept getting drawn back into [conversations](http://blog.ianbicking.org/nonlinear-learning-nonlinear-internet.html) around [OLPC](http://laptop.org), until I realized that's exactly what I should be writing about!
