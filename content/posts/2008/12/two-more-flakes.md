Blizzard")](http://flickr.com/photos/mccloud/99089480/)6 credits and another season later, I have two more essays to show for the time indentured to my phd [program](http://collectivecommunicationscampus.net/). One of these years I might even save up enough flakes for a snow bank.
I had fun with this one, which I wrote for a [class](http://jonahboss.fastmail.fm/school/Hist%20of%20Theory%20of%20Arch/syllabus_1.pdf) on the History of the Theory of Architecture - the [assignment](http://jonahboss.fastmail.fm/school/Hist%20of%20Theory%20of%20Arch/midterm.pdf) was to analyze a piece of architectural theory, so naturally I chose an information architect...
*[Possibility Spaces](http://pocketknowledge.tc.columbia.edu/home.php/viewfile/69866): Architecture and the Builders of Information Societies*
This other paper was for my [seminar](http://jonahboss.fastmail.fm/school/j6019/j6019_transparency_syllabus.doc) with [Michael Schudson](http://www.journalism.columbia.edu/cs/ContentServer/jrn/1165270051276/JRN_Profile_C/1165270082820/JRNFacultyDetail.htm) on Transparency and Democracy. It packages up some thinking I have been doing for a while on the politics of memory, surveillance, and transparency, and opens up some serious ground for future research.
*[The End of Forgetting](http://pocketknowledge.tc.columbia.edu/home.php/viewfile/69867): Transparent Identities and Permanent Records*
Next stop is a week in Vermont - off the grid (honestly, its almost off the map), but am already looking forward to next Spring's semester, kicking off with this [conference](http://www.journalism.columbia.edu/cs/ContentServer/jrn/1212610477235/page/1212610471757/simplepage.htm) on The Changing Dynamics of Public Controversies.
