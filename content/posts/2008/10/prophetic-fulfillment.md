It is virtually uncontested that the McCain campaign has attempted to [divisively identify](http://www.time.com/time/politics/article/0,8599,1830590,00.html) Obama as the Anti-Christ through a systematic campaign of allusions and coded associations. This innuendo was largely missed by people who don't believe in the literal reading of Revelations, but the sophisticated tactics make it unlikely the [multitude of associations](http://religionblog.dallasnews.com/archives/2008/08/behold-his-mighty-hand-is-the.html) were coincidental. "[The One](http://www.livingroomcandidate.org/commercials/2008)" advertisement alludes to the cover art and even the title fonts of the popular "[Left Behind](http://www.leftbehind.com/)" series, and there are numerous biblical associations as well.
But, what confuses me is that by the logic of fundamentalist Christianity, if Obama really were "The One", wouldn't they be obliged to vote for him to fulfil prophesy and usher in the rapture? Isn't this the logic behind the Christian right's support for Israel? Kinda reminds me of seating Jesus on a white donkey, but really, whatever it takes to bring about a change we can all believe in...
