The software was initially created by free software developers who wanted control over their own manuals, but has spread beyond manuals and software. Last year I was actually involved, from afar, in another booki project - [Collaborative Futures](http://collaborative-futures.org/) - but, I had not actually sprinted on that book and did not have any direct experience using booki. Intriguingly, a cultural practice has sprung up around booki, the book sprint.
