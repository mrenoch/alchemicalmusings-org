I apologize for this study blog's late start - I just returned from the [Plone conference in Vienna](http://ploneconf2005.bluedynamics.net/), and the internet availability was spottier than it should have been.
At the conference I presented a talk which relates closely to the topic of this seminar, entitled [Platonic Wikis and Subversive Social Interfaces](http://ploneconf2005.bluedynamics.net/speakers/jonah-bossewitch). People seemed very interested in the subject, and a common response was that these ideas were obvious when stated, but people were very happy to hear them concisely articulated and formulated.
I will be posting my slides up on the conference site, but in the meantime, here is a working link to them: [html](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/draft/jonah/vienna2005/Platonic-Wikis.htm) [ppt](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/draft/jonah/vienna2005/Platonic-Wikis.ppt) Photos and links from the conference should start appearing under plonecon2005 over the next few days.
I will be catching up with ss05, blog postings, and sleep this weekend.
