[![escher_hands.jpg](/images/2007/09/escher_hands.jpg)](http://alchemicalmusings.org/2007/09/06/promiscuous-laptops/escher_handsjpg/ "escher_hands.jpg")So, I published another post on OLPCNews today:
[Sharing and Not-Sharing Alike User Generated Content](http://www.olpcnews.com/content/education/sharing_share_alike_user_content.html)
I am trying to figure out the best way to aggregate my own work, and am a little stumped. On the one hand, I don't want to duplicate content, but on the other, I am skeptical of the long term prospects of some of the sites I have contributed to. Guess that's what happens when [you don't own your own data](http://alchemicalmusings.org/2006/03/12/saints-in-the-church-of-writely/).
Anyway, I am starting to at least keep a [running list of links](http://alchemicalmusings.org/publications/) to this kind of stuff. I know these aren't all traditional "publications", but it is important that people start regarding some of these kinds of contributions along these lines.
