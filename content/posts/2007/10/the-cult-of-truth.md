Last week I heard Ron Suskind speak at the J-School. He is a pulitzer prize winning investigative journalist who broken stories on the inner workings of the current administration.
He describes the "cult of message", and talks about how its as if the new rulebook was worked on in an R&D shop.
http://www.journalism.columbia.edu/cs/ContentServer/jrn/1165270052298/JRN\_News\_C/1175372937682/JRNNewsDetail.htm
The question for me is whether or not the old rulebook is up to the task of taking on the new one without any revisions of its own.
What if you broke the story of the century and nobody cared?
http://alchemicalmusings.org/2005/10/07/serenity-lost/
I've got nothing against the truth - I just think it might need a bit of TLC in the face of [lovemarks](http://www.lovemarks.com/) and .... and all.
